Hi, I’m Mali. After a decade of full-stack engineering, the problems worth solving have shifted. It’s no longer about how fast we can ship a feature, design an API, or fix a bug. It’s about understanding where the systems we build break when we ship software at 10x the speed.

I've always been driven by building products that people love. That will never change. But as the cost of building software falls, the quality, security, and reliability of what we put into the world must not fall with it.

Applying my engineering background to application security is where I can have the most impact. The hardest problems are now centered on building systems people can genuinely rely on, because to build products people love, we have to build products that earn their trust.
