Hi, I'm Mali. If a decade of software engineering has taught me anything, it's that I care about solving hard, meaningful problems. And the truth is... churning out AI-generated code doesn't scratch that itch anymore.

The hard problems have shifted. It's no longer about how quickly we can build a feature, design an API, or fix a bug. It's about understanding where things break when we ship at 10x the speed, and how those failures affect the people who trust our products.

I've always been driven by building products that people love. That will never change. But as the cost of building software falls, the quality, security and reliability of what we put into the world mustn’t fall with it. 

That's what draws me to the intersection of Product Engineering and Application Security. I'm looking to apply my full-stack engineering background to building products that remain trustworthy in the age of AI.
